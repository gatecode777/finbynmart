# Pages 25-26 — Buyer Settings & Notifications: implementation report

**Status:** Implemented and verified on the throwaway database; QA not complete. **BLOCKED / NOT AVAILABLE:** two-step verification, login alerts, email/SMS/WhatsApp notifications, promotional and recommendation preferences (no consent system), profile visibility, date/number format, business preferences, integrations, data export and account deletion (support-handled), device list, the email-verified notification, quotation/order/supplier-reply notifications (no such events).

**Audit:** `docs/backend/PAGE_25_26_SETTINGS_NOTIFICATIONS_AUDIT.md`.
**Frontend:** `src/app/buyer/settings/page.tsx`; `src/components/buyer/settings/*` (frame and tabs, preferences form, sign-out-everywhere, not-available lists, notification centre, bell with popup panel); shared `src/components/ui/Toast/*` (toast area, flash helpers) mounted in the root layout; shell changes: gold active sidebar item, icon-only top bar, sidebar minimized to icons and widening (pushing the content) on hover or focus, no sidebar toggle, no profile block at the top of the sidebar; header account link re-reads the session after navigation and auth events and links buyers to My Dashboard (no sign-out in the marketplace header for buyers; suppliers keep it).
**Backend:** `src/lib/buyer/{settingsRules,settings,notificationRules,notifications}.ts`; routes `/api/buyer/settings`, `/api/buyer/settings/sign-out-everywhere`, `/api/buyer/notifications`, `/unread-count`, `/[id]/read`, `/read-all`; `bumpSessionVersion` in `users.ts`; events hooked into inquiry submission, company document upload and change password; the dashboard reads the saved period.
**Schema:** new `buyer_settings` (unique per buyer) and `notifications` (four newest-first indexes with `_id` tie-breaker, unique event key, 180-day TTL); `security_events` reused.
**Behaviour:** only real settings are switches; required `account` notices cannot be switched off; preferences applied at creation on the server; idempotent events from genuine actions only; safe links only; mark-all in bounded batches of 200.
**Results:** 15/15 unit, 91/91 browser and API (performance skipped) plus the performance group (see below), Page 22 96/96, Page 23 102/102, Page 24 85/85.

Details and causes in `PAGE_25_26_PERFORMANCE_REPORT.md`.
