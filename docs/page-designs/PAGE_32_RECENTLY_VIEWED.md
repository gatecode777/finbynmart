## Identity and resolution
The key is the one canonical product key of the site (`product.id`: catalogue id, sample slug, or approved supplier product `sp-<id>`), validated by `resolveProduct` on record and resolved for display by `getPublicProductByKey` (in-memory maps; eligibility including active category and approved status is re-applied on every read). Supplier products are not excluded (lead matching's catalogue restriction does not apply). The client sends only `{ productId }`.

## Recording rules
Only the client recorder records, after the product page has displayed: it reads `/api/auth/me` (same call as the header), posts only for role `buyer`, once per product per mount, and ignores any failure. Server rendering, GETs, prefetch, listings, search, invalid or unpublished products (404 from `resolveProduct`), visitors, suppliers and admins record nothing. The product page stays cached and user-independent. No anonymous queue and no merging of device history.

## Storage, limits, pagination
Collection `recently_viewed`: `{ userId, productKey, lastViewedAt }` only. Indexes: unique `recent_per_buyer_product (userId, productKey)`; `recent_newest (userId, lastViewedAt -1, _id -1)`. Defaults (implementation choices, not business policy): keep 100 per buyer (oldest trimmed after a record), 20 per load, no time expiry. Listing is cursor-based on `(lastViewedAt, _id)` (opaque `cursor`, `limit` up to 20), one query of limit+1 rows, then in-memory resolution; the whole history is never loaded. Unavailable policy: an entry whose product is no longer public stays as a neutral "Product no longer available" card with no name, link or supplier detail and a remove button.

## API
`GET /api/buyer/recently-viewed?cursor=&limit=`; `POST` `{productId}`; `DELETE` (clear own history); `DELETE /api/buyer/recently-viewed/[entryId]`. Buyer role from the session (`requireBuyerApi`); no buyer id is ever read from the request; writes call `isCrossSiteWrite`, require `application/json`, at most 500 characters and only `productId`; rate limits: read 120 per 10 min (reserved), record 120, remove 120, clear 20 (exact `rateLimit` for writes); responses `private, no-store`; `{ ok, message, fields }` errors; 503 with a safe message on failure; remove and clear are idempotent and no success is returned before the write.

## Concurrency
Record = one upsert with `$max: lastViewedAt` (concurrent views make one row; a duplicate-key race is retried once; a late older write cannot move an entry back). The cap trim deletes entries beyond 100 in index order; simultaneous trims are idempotent and history can briefly exceed 100 under heavy concurrency until the next record. Remove = `deleteOne(_id, userId)`. Clear = `deleteMany(userId)`; a view recorded during a clear may land before or after it, so clearing never promises to stop later recording. No transaction is used.

## UI states
Loading: skeleton cards (reduced-motion safe). Populated: newest first, one entry per product, "Load more" with de-duplication. Empty: "No recently viewed products yet." / "Explore products to start your history." / Browse Products (`/products`). Load failure: "We couldn't load your recently viewed products." with Retry (never the empty state). Remove and Clear are not optimistic: entries change only after the server confirms; failures keep the entries, show an inline alert and an error toast. Clear opens the shared alert dialog (focus starts on Cancel, Escape cancels, explains that only this history is cleared; synchronous ref guards make a double click send one request). Remove buttons are labelled with the product name. An auth-change event drops the list and reloads the page. The design board's sample labels and empty-state inset are not rendered.
